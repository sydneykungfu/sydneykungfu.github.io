Frequently Asked Questions

When will the Chatswood class start?

We'll set the day, time, and venue based on the interest we receive. Register above and we'll contact you as soon as the class is confirmed.

Does registering commit me to anything?

No. Registering your interest is free and has no obligation — it simply helps us plan the class around the people who want to attend.

Can I train now while I wait?

Yes. You're welcome to join our existing classes in Marsfield and Hornsby — see the adult classes and kids classes.

What style of Kung Fu do you teach?

We teach traditional Choy Lee Fut, a Southern Chinese martial art known for its powerful strikes and fluid footwork, alongside Choi Mok Pai techniques.
