Web Marketing Performance

Web Marketing Performance is an internal marketing analytics tool built and used by Tse Kung Fu Academy (sydneykungfu.au) to review our own SEO, website traffic, and advertising performance in one place.

The app connects to our own Google Search Console, Google Analytics, and Google Ads accounts to read read-only performance data — such as search rankings, site traffic, and ad campaign results — so we can understand how our marketing is performing and make better decisions about our own website and classes.

This is a private, single-user tool for our own internal use. It is not a public product or service.
